**Scoring protocol v1.0 (decided 2026-10-24, before any MICA result exists; the chain counts of the batch-1 baseline models had been seen, which is why the fragment-agnostic variant 2 is in)**
What the evidence says: (a) the MICA paper (bioRxiv 2025.07.03.663071, text read on 2026-10-24) normalises TM-score "by the length of the true structure", takes TM-score, aligned length and sequence identity from US-align (earlier version: "options for aligning multi-chain oligomeric structures and all chains"), defines Calpha match as the percentage of predicted Calpha atoms within a distance threshold of true Calpha atoms (precision), coverage as predicted residues / residues of the true structure, and cites an earlier paper for the Calpha quality score without giving the formula in that text; (b) US-align `-mm 1` (tested on 2026-10-24 with synthetic models) pairs chains one to one, ignores extra copies in the prediction (TM-score normalised by the deposited model: 0.940 with three copies, 0.947 with one) but counts only ONE fragment per deposited chain (a model cut into 7 fragments: 0.14 instead of 0.94); (c) the batch-1 models: ModelAngelo's final model is cut into 2 to 24 "chains" (fragments), EMProt writes all copies of the map into one long chain (9T5K 2182 residues, 22FX 4340 for 173 deposited), E3-CryoFold and EModelX(+AF) write 1 to 3 chains of about the deposited size; (d) `phenix.chain_comparison` has no superposition step and reads CLOSE/FAR as counts of query residues (CLOSE + FAR = residues of the query).
Protocol (the same for every method and entry; implemented in `scripts/score_models.py`, 2 US-align runs and 2 Phenix runs per model):
1. **Deposited model**: RCSB mmCIF, first model, amino-acid residues only (no water, ligands, nucleic acids), first alternative location. **Prediction**: the same cleaning.
2. **TM-score and its companions (primary, as in the paper)**: `USalign pred native -mm 1 -ter 1 -mol protein -outfmt 2 -m matrix` (prediction = structure 1). `tm_score` = TM2 (normalised by the deposited model), `aligned_ca_length` = Lali, `ca_rmsd` = RMSD, `seq_identity` = IDali; `tm_score_pred_norm` = TM1 is kept for information. Extra copies do not lower `tm_score`; a model cut into fragments is penalised (one fragment per deposited chain), which is a property of its output and is reported as such.
3. **Fragment-agnostic TM-score (sensitivity analysis, reported next to the primary one)**: all protein chains of the prediction go into one chain and all chains of the deposited model into one chain (file order), `USalign ... -mm 5 -ter 2 -mol protein -outfmt 2` (fully non-sequential alignment): `tm_score_fns`, `aligned_ca_length_fns`, `ca_rmsd_fns`, `seq_identity_fns`. Fragment order, chain breaks and extra copies do not matter. It is more generous than the primary score; no conclusion is drawn that holds under only one of the two without saying so.
4. **Calpha match, sequence match, Calpha quality score**: `phenix.chain_comparison <deposited> <prediction>` with default parameters (3.0 A, protein, `test_unique_part_of_target_only=True`), on the prediction **superposed onto the deposited model by the matrix of the `-mm 1` run** ("superposed", primary; the maps hold symmetry copies of the deposited model, a method may build a copy other than the deposited one, and a rigid superposition costs nothing when the model is already in place), and on the prediction in its own frame ("frame fixed", secondary). From the table row: `ca_match` = 100 * CLOSE N / (CLOSE N + FAR N) (the paper's definition), `ca_found` = % FOUND (reported as it is printed; the share of the deposited residues that were found, not used in a product), `seq_match` = SEQ MATCH(%), `ca_rmsd_close` = RMSD of the close residues. `coverage` = Calpha residues of the prediction / Calpha residues of the deposited model (the paper's definition, not capped, so it is above 1 for a model that holds more copies than the deposited one). `ca_quality` = `ca_match` * `coverage` (SPEC decision 7); when CLOSE + FAR = residues of the prediction this equals 100 * CLOSE N / deposited residues, so extra copies cancel out of it. `ca_match` alone is lowered by built copies that are not in the deposited model: it is reported as defined, with the number of copies (`coverage`) next to it, and conclusions rest on `tm_score` and `ca_quality`.
5. **Own check**: the same counts (CLOSE, FAR, found, sequence matches of the close residues) from a nearest-neighbour search (`own_*` columns). They are the fallback when Phenix cannot be used and a check of the parsing; they are never mixed with the Phenix columns.
6. **Which file is the model of a method (primary)**: ModelAngelo `<entry>_modelangelo.cif` (sequence-assigned; `_raw` secondary), E3-CryoFold `pre_align` output, EMProt `output.cif` (`output_denovo.cif` secondary), EModelX(+AF) `all_atom_model.pdb` (`ca_model.pdb` secondary), MICA `MICA_all_atom_model.pdb` (`ca_model`, `real_space_refined` and the other files secondary; chosen before any MICA result: the program's own output, available even when the background refinement is not). All files are scored and kept in `scores_long.csv`; the tables and tests of decision 8 use the primary files.
7. A model that cannot be read or has no amino-acid residue is `no_model`; a US-align run that fails or runs out of time (15 minutes) is `no_alignment` (numbers empty, reason in `note`). Neither is dropped: both count as failures in the sensitivity analysis (score 0).
8. The statistics of decision 8 (paired Wilcoxon on `tm_score`, paired t-test, bootstrap, Holm) are computed by `scripts/analyze_results.py` from `scores_long.csv` and the status tables; the failure analysis uses 0 for every metric.

Each session has a budget in hours (default 9, the first cell): no run starts that cannot end in time (1.25 times the mean time of its method), programs still running 10 minutes before the budget ends are stopped (`interrupted`), what is left is in `pending.csv`. Nothing is dropped because of its results.

7. The scores of the notebook are a first pass (US-align of chain against chain, `modeled_residues` as the denominator). The final metrics (decision 7: multi-chain US-align, `chain_comparison`, coverage, completion) are computed from the models that every zip keeps, after the protocol for entries with several copies is settled.
