\section{Datasets}\label{sec:datasets}

\subsection{Selection of the entries}
The test set was fixed before any method was run (spec decisions 3 to 5; the entry list was committed on 16~October 2026). An entry qualifies when its EMDB map and its PDB model were both released between 1~January and 12~October 2026, so that no method can have been tuned on it by the authors of the maps. The release date of every map was checked in the EMDB, and the pool was frozen on 12~October. The filters were applied to the metadata and the files of the deposition:

\begin{itemize}
\item single-particle reconstruction, resolution of 4.0~\AA{} or better, one EMDB map per entry;
\item protein only (no nucleic-acid chain), 1 to 700 modelled residues in total, at most 3 unique chain sequences, at most 5\,\% UNK residues;
\item a map--model correlation CC$_\text{mask}$ of at least \SelCcMin{}, computed for every entry in the same way with Phenix \texttt{map\_model\_cc} at the resolution of the entry.
\end{itemize}
Table~\ref{tab:flow} (Appendix) shows how the pool of \PoolAll{} cryo-EM entries released in the window shrinks to \PoolFinal{} candidates. The candidates were clustered with MMseqs2 \citep{steinegger2017mmseqs2} at 30\,\% sequence identity on the longest chain of each entry (\SelClusters{} clusters), so that no protein family is used twice. Two strata were defined by resolution (at most 3.0~\AA{}, and above 3.0 up to 4.0~\AA{}). In each stratum the candidates were put in a random order (seed \SelSeed{}) and the first \SelLow{} that passed every filter were taken; an entry whose cluster or map was already used was skipped. The CC$_\text{mask}$ rule removed \SelCcRejected{} entries (all in the lower-resolution stratum) and \SelCcFailed{} more because Phenix could not compute it (a charged-oxygen atom type that its scattering table does not know). The seed, the shuffle and every skipped entry are recorded in the selection log of the repository.

\subsection{The twenty entries}
The final list has \NEntries{} entries, \SelLow{} per stratum (Table~\ref{tab:entries} in the Appendix). Resolutions range from \ResMin{} to \ResMax{}~\AA{}; the deposited models hold \ResiduesMin{} to \ResiduesMax{} residues (median \ResiduesMedian{}); \NSingleChain{} entries have one protein chain and \NMultiChain{} have several chains, some of them copies. The maps range from 16~MB to 1~GB. Because the rule takes one entry per cluster and the seeded order decides which one, the list favours common specimens only to the extent that the pool does (seven clusters hold a quarter of the candidates); the cluster sizes of the chosen entries are in the repository.

\paragraph{How new is the protein?}
The maps are new, but the proteins often are not. For every unique chain we recorded the highest sequence identity to any chain of a PDB entry released before 2026 (MMseqs2 search against \texttt{pdb\_seqres}, alignments covering at least half of the chain). \NIdentHigh{} entries have a chain with 95\,\% identity or more to an older entry (for example 22FX, human apoferritin), \NIdentMid{} lie between 44 and 76\,\%, and only \NIdentLow{} (9TKA, 9VBD and 26DK) are at 0\,\%. Training data of the methods are not known in detail, so a method may have seen a homologue of a test protein. The planned sensitivity analysis on the subset at 30\,\% identity or less has \NIdentLow{} entries, too few for a test; it is described in Section~\ref{sec:results} and not tested.

\subsection{AlphaFold~3 inputs}
One AF3 prediction was made for each of the \AfChains{} unique chain sequences of the \NEntries{} entries with AlphaFold Server (default settings, random seed, no custom templates); the first model (\texttt{model\_0}) is used by every method that takes predicted structures. The mean pLDDT of the predictions ranges from \AfPlddtMin{} to \AfPlddtMax{} (median \AfPlddtMed{}); the chains with most residues below 50 are \AfLowList{}. A nine-residue peptide with an unknown first residue (9V4D chain~E2) was submitted with glycine in its place. Each prediction was checked against its submitted sequence. The predictions are not published in the repository (the Server terms allow non-commercial research use only); the job list, the checks and the file hashes are.
